## Security reports

Do not disclose credentials, private investor data, or an exploitable vulnerability in a public issue. Use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting surface when it is available for the repository; otherwise contact the repository owner privately through the profile linked from [GitHub](https://github.com/suvimatt).

For ordinary defects and feature requests, use [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/suvimatt/agentic-thesis/issues).

## Trust boundary

AgenticThesis processes untrusted filings, issuer pages, uploaded documents, and model output. Its safety boundary depends on:

- preserving original bytes and provenance before interpretation;
- treating vector data as a rebuildable index, not authoritative state;
- validating exact evidence locations and quotes;
- keeping model output non-authoritative until Human Review;
- preventing stale approvals from overwriting newer thesis history;
- serving stored artifacts as downloads rather than executable browser content.

## Investment boundary

The project maintains company-fundamentals theses. It does not provide personalized investment advice, determine fair value, size positions, or decide whether to buy, sell, or hold a security. Source evidence may be incomplete or wrong, model output may be wrong, and Human Review remains mandatory.
